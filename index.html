Expense Tracker

A simple monthly expense tracker for Android.

Expense Tracker helps you record what you spend and see where your money goes, month by month.

What it does

  • Add, edit and delete expenses and income, with category, payment method, date and note.
  • See a monthly summary, a spending-by-category chart and a six-month trend.
  • Set optional monthly and per-category budgets.
  • Works offline: your data is stored on your device.
  • Optional backup: connect your Google account and the app keeps a copy of your expenses in a Google Sheet in your own Google Drive.

How it uses your Google account

If you choose to connect Google, the app uses your Google sign-in to create and update one spreadsheet, called "Expense Tracker Data", in your Google Drive. It requests only the drive.file permission, which lets the app see and change only the files it creates itself, and nothing else in your Drive.
