Privacy Policy

Expense Tracker ("the app") is developed by Deepanshu Garg. Last updated: 8 November 2026.

What the app stores

  • Your expense data: the amounts, dates, categories, payment methods, notes and budgets you enter. This is stored in a database on your own device.
  • Google account information (only if you connect Google): your email address, used to show which account is connected.

How the app uses Google user data

If you tap "Connect Google account", the app asks for the Google Drive permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. The app uses it only to:

  • create one spreadsheet named "Expense Tracker Data" in your Google Drive, and
  • write your expenses into that spreadsheet as a backup copy.

This permission only lets the app access files that the app itself created. The app cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.

What we do not do

  • The developer does not run a server for this app and does not receive, collect or have access to your data.
  • Your data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train any model.
  • The app does not use analytics or advertising services.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

  • Delete expenses inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove the data stored on your device.
  • Delete the "Expense Tracker Data" spreadsheet in Google Drive to remove the backup copy.
  • Revoke the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or tap "Disconnect" in the app's Settings.

Changes and contact
